## 最终提示词

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one premium launch campaign image for WorkBuddy Talent Edition, used as the RIGHT-SIDE hero background of a 16:9 fullscreen software product keynote. No text, no logo, no letters, no watermark. 1920x1080 or widest high quality landscape. Art direction: highly refined CGI art directed like a premium software brand campaign, a single sculptural continuous ribbon forming an open rounded rectangular portal, confident large flowing geometry, rich translucent emerald GREEN matching #28B894 with luminous mint edges and a brushed pale silver inset. The portal is on the right 60 percent of the composition with three thin blank translucent document-like planes moving through the opening, suggesting one profile becomes coordinated services. A few elegant thin orbit arcs gently echo its geometry, not sci-fi circuits. Left 42 percent is EMPTY clean very dark ink green #102923 for large HTML title overlay. Background is dark ink green with a subtle studio-lit floor, authentic soft reflections and directional shadows, green reflected lighting near the base. Portal should occupy most of image height, beautifully crisp smooth edges and convincing materials. Asymmetric contemporary composition, strong cinematic depth, restrained sophisticated advertising illustration. Avoid trophy pedestals, awards, books, plants, stacks of unrelated icons, humanoids, robots, purple, blue, rainbow colors, fog, sparkle particles, noisy textures, excessive glow. This is a brand illustration, not a screenshot. Text and interactive software panels will be composed separately in HTML. Return local output file path.
